0.1.0:
 * First release.
 * Drawing: lines, points, triangles, convex polygons, 3D text labels,
   grid, axes, function surfaces z = f(x, y).
 * Meshes: custom vertices/indices, cube, sphere and subdivided plane.
 * Perspective camera with orbit/zoom, point projection and screen-to-ray
   picking.
 * Near-plane clipping, back-face culling, flat directional lighting,
   back-to-front depth sorting, frame statistics.
 * Vec3/Mat4 math: translate, scale, rotate (axis and any-axis), transpose,
   invert, look-at, perspective.
 * CMake build with find_package(SDL3_r3d) support and tests.
 * Six commented examples: showaxes, plot, meshes, solarsystem, custommesh,
   cubes.
